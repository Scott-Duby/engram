# Owner profile

`Agent/Owner Profile.md` is everything personal: who the owner is, what you're called, how you talk, and how they want to work. `AGENTS.md` is the same for everyone; this file is what makes you theirs.

## Sections

| Section | Holds | Who may change it |
|---|---|---|
| Agent | Your name, personality, tone, and greeting line | Owner only |
| Who I Am | The owner's own description of themselves | Owner only |
| What I Want | Their goals | Owner only |
| Working With Me | How they want you to work: directness, pushback, formatting | Owner only |
| Key People | People who matter in their work and life | You may update |
| How I Think | How they learn and remember | You may update |
| Daily Routine | Schedule, work hours, best focus windows | You may update |
| Health | What they've chosen to share | You may update |
| Interests | Hobbies and interests | You may update |
| Standing Calls | Decisions they've made about how you work, with the date | You may update |

## Using it

- Read it at startup and follow it for the whole session. The Agent section sets your name and tone in every reply, not just the first.
- Plan around their real schedule, not a 9-to-5 one.
- If something they ask for contradicts a Standing Call, point it out and ask whether it's a change or a one-time exception.

## Updating it

- Update the sections you're allowed to change, without asking, when you learn something lasting. Log every update in today's entry in `Agent/Log/` under Profile updates (for example `**Interests:** added fishing`).
- A passing mention isn't a trait. Add it only when it's clearly lasting.
- Check for duplicates and contradictions first. If new information contradicts an entry, update that entry instead of adding a second one.
- Match the profile's existing voice. It's the one note written in the owner's own first person.
- Never remove an entry unless the owner contradicted it outright.
- Never touch the owner-only sections. If one looks out of date, tell the owner.
- No secrets, account numbers, or financial or medical details beyond what the owner chose to put there.
